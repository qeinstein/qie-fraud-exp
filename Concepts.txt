We will be focusing solely on two Concepts from Quantum computing to come up with this algorithm

1. Continuous-Time Quantum Walk (CTQW) Hamiltonian Dynamics
2. Quantum-Inspired Spectral Feature Map (Unitary Wave Operator Embedding)


Dataset for this experiment
    - public Elliptic Bitcoin Dataset (or an AMLSim synthetic transaction graph). Elliptic contains 203,769 node transactions and 234,355 directed edges with labeled
    illicit vs. licit transactions under real-world class imbalance (~2% illicit).

Baselines to beat(Production grade classical systems to beat):
 - Standard Tabular Random Forest / XGBoost on raw node features.
- 2-hop and 4-hop Classical GCN / GraphSAGE (to capture the exact point where GNN performance collapses due to over-smoothing).

Primary Target Metrics:
- PR-AUC (Precision-Recall AUC)
- F1-Score on the minority (illicit) class
- False Positive Rate at 95% Recall.
